Lo que dice

Una línea al azar cada vez que apretás E (nunca la misma dos veces seguidas). Se muestran en inglés, como en el juego.

Two wheels or two wheels with boxes. Both cost more than walking. Walking costs more than you think.
The cargo bike's slower and thirstier. It also brings home what you went out for.
Ran dry out past the gate? My crane hauls it back. Thirty in the hundred of what it's worth, coins on the counter.
I don't take the bank's word for anything. Coins, here, in my hand.
That one on the wall? First bike I ever fixed. It doesn't run. Neither did I, back then.
Every bike I sell comes back on the hook sooner or later. I make money both ways.
