La oferta

Una línea al azar cada vez que apretás E (nunca la misma dos veces seguidas). Se muestran en inglés, como en el juego.

New here? Fresh boots, clean hands, and nothing on your back. That won't last a minute down the shaft. Want me to walk you through your kit?

“Show me.” / “Later.”

Pasos

  1. Abrí los cajones de recién llegado del palé

    Abrir: Supply Crate, Gear Crate · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Two crates on the pallet by the landing, both yours. Supply's got a pack and work clothes, Gear's got the rest. Click one to open it.

  2. Ponete la mochila

    Equipar: Mochila · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Pack goes on first. Without it you've four pockets; with it, twenty cells and more weight on your back. Double-click it in the crate.

  3. Ponete el Leather Suit y el casco

    Equipar: Torso, Piernas, Cabeza · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Now the Leather Suit, jacket and trousers, and the helmet. Armour takes the edge off every hit. Boots too, if you've any sense.

  4. Colocá la batería

    Equipar: Batería · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Battery cell's in the Gear crate. It's your energy, the blue gauge under your health. Lamps and powers drink from it, and nothing down there tops it up.

  5. Colgá la linterna y encendela (T)

    Luz encendida (T) · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Hang the lantern on, then T to light it. Light costs charge, so kill it when you don't need it. But in the dark, you'll need it.

  6. Tomá tu arma

    Equipar: Arma principal · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Your weapon's in there too. Every hit trains its skill, K shows you how far along. C is your trade's own power, Q a quick dash out of trouble.

  7. Poné el taladro en la ranura de herramienta (mantené F para perforar)

    Equipar: Herramienta · marcado por el cartel «NEWCOMER CRATES»

    Last, the drill. Tool slot. Stand at a rock face and hold F. Ore pays, and rock is the only thing down there that doesn't fight back.

  8. Volvé a hablar con Old Brannock

    Hablar con Old Brannock · Old Brannock aparece resaltado

    Well, look at you. Pack, suit, light, weapon, drill. Almost look like you belong here.

    “All kitted out.”

  9. Bajá en el elevador del pozo (G en la cabina)

    Llegar a The Threshold

    The lift's the car in the well, up the landing. Walk onto it, press G. The Threshold's first. Stay in the light, and come back up with something.

Recompensas

60 XP al terminar: 60% de lo que pide el nivel 2.
