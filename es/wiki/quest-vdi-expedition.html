La oferta

Una línea al azar cada vez que apretás E (nunca la misma dos veces seguidas). Se muestran en inglés, como en el juego.

Operator. Vantage Deep Industries extends a trial contract: one Expedition Service, no charge. Use it on open ground and a VDI drillship comes down for you. Terms: you report the result.

“I'll take the trial.” / “Not now.”

Pasos

  1. Retirá el Expedition Service de prueba con el agente de VDI

    Hablar con Vdi Field Unit · Vdi Field Unit aparece resaltado

    Issuing one Expedition Service. It is a tablet; keep it in your cargo. It is spent when the ship is called.

    “Hand it over.”

  2. Llamá al drillship en el claro de relevamiento de VDI sobre Frontier Road, al sur de Dustline

    call_out

    Leave by the Frontier Gate and walk south onto Frontier Road. VDI keeps a survey clearing off the bay by the Dustline gate, past the Castoff stair pit; the board marks it. Any open ground off the road would do, away from gates, wrecks and stair pits. Use the Expedition Service there (double-click it, or put it on a hotkey). The ship lands thirteen metres ahead of you. Do not stand under it.

  3. Contale el llamado al agente de VDI en Dustline

    Hablar con Vdi Field Unit · Vdi Field Unit aparece resaltado

    Call-out logged. The ship is yours while it stands: its door opens to the control room, the terminal by the door runs the bore. Load batteries and gas cans before you go deep. Further Expedition Services: ten thousand Metal, at this unit.

    “It's down. It's mine.”

Recompensas

150 XP al terminar: 150% de lo que pide el nivel 2.
