Faction

The Burrowborn

Organic + mineral + subterranean

Dark wet hide, pale exposed tissue, mineral plates and a faint green glow. Eyes are small or absent.

  • Territorial
  • Pack-minded
  • Senses vibration and heat
  • Aggressive, not clever
Communication
Vibration through rock: a hurt Burrowborn calls its kind nearby.
Territory
Nests and tunnels in the Shallows; they rarely chase far from home.
Hierarchy
Swarm → Brute → Alpha → Deepmaw

How to fight it

  • The end of the Shallows belongs to it: it waits in the east chamber in front of the way down to C-3.
  • Keep your distance and it charges: a red lane lights up in front of it — step off the lane.

How to fight it

  • It hunts: once it has your scent it follows you far beyond its gallery.
  • Close in it bites; step away from it and it spits fire along the ground — the line glows before it does.
  • Its scorching breath fans out in front of it: get behind it or behind stone. Then it backs off to spit again.
  • Leave it alone and it stops to lick its wounds: keep hitting it and the healing never comes.
