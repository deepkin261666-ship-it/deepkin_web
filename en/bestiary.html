The Burrowborn

Organic + mineral + subterranean

Dark wet hide, pale exposed tissue, mineral plates and a faint green glow. Eyes are small or absent.

  • Territorial
  • Pack-minded
  • Senses vibration and heat
  • Aggressive, not clever
Communication
Vibration through rock: a hurt Burrowborn calls its kind nearby.
Territory
Nests and tunnels in the Shallows; they rarely chase far from home.
