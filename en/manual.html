Newcomers

Your first descent

  1. Open your crates

    Two newcomer crates wait in the Outpost. The Supply Crate has a backpack, the Leather Suit and a K6-3 helmet; the Gear Crate has your starting weapon (and rounds), a lantern, a battery and the Big Daddy drill. What's inside is yours alone.

  2. Equip everything

    Press I and drag each item onto its slot. Battery first: without it, your lantern and abilities have no energy.

  3. Try the drill

    Two drill sites sit by the shaft. Hold F at one and watch the rock give way.

  4. Take the lift

    Press G at the Shaft 3 elevator. The Threshold is quiet; The Shallows are not.

  5. Watch your battery

    Your lantern drains energy and nothing recharges it out there. Light it when you need to see, turn it off when you don't.

Character

Attributes

Four attributes, set by your class. Level does not inflate them: your gear and your training do the growing.

Might
Melee damage, stagger and carry.
Endurance
Health, stamina and resistance.
Precision
Ranged damage, accuracy and weak points.
Technology
Cheaper abilities and energy use.

How it adds up

Health = 76 + 4 × Endurance

Stamina = 5 × Endurance

Attribute bonus = +1.5% per point above 10 (−25% … +25%)

Melee uses Might · Ranged uses Precision

Power cost −1.5% per Technology above 10 (max −30%)

Combat

Damage & defense

Your hit

weapon base × class coefficient × attribute bonus × skill bonus × ammo

Each class has a melee, ranged, ability and defense coefficient. Foundry swings at ×1.2 but shoots at ×0.6; Drift is the reverse.

Damage calculator

Their hit

reduction = rating ÷ (rating + 100), max 75%

Armor counts against physical damage, resistance against everything else. 100 armor halves a physical hit.

Foundry starts at 63 armor (−39%). Relay starts at 3.

Training

Weapon skills

Skills belong to weapon families, not classes. Every hit that lands trains the family of the weapon that dealt it.

  • BladesSwords and axes. Chained hits build Heat: +2, +4, +6 damage within 3 s.
  • BluntHammers and maces. Slow, heavy blows. Later: staggering and breaking armour and rock.
  • Short BladesKnives and daggers. Ambush: +4 damage after moving 2 m since your last hit. Later: silent takedowns.
  • FirearmsShotguns, carbines and rifles. Steady fire at mid range.
  • PrecisionPrecision rifles. +3 damage on targets 6 m or more away. Later: weak points.
  • SporesSpore projectors. With one in hand, Bloom poisons enemies instead of healing.
  • VitalismLiving weapons that grow with their bearer. Later: stronger healing.
  • TechEnergy weapons and field electronics. Later: hacking terminals, doors, turrets and drones.
  • ShieldingTrained by taking hits with a shield up: 8% less damage, up to 40%. Later: active block and parry.

10 skill XP per hit, 8 per hit blocked with a shield. Your class affinity multiplies it (×1.5 for its specialties, ×0.5 for families it has no feel for). Each skill level adds +1% damage with that family, up to level 100.

Survival

Light & energy

Your battery sets your energy. The lantern drains it every second it is on, and abilities spend it too. There is no recharging on an expedition: when it hits zero, the light goes out.

Toggle the lantern with T. Unequipping the battery empties your energy; equipping it again does not refill it.

Simple Lantern: 9 m radius · 0.15/s · ≈ 11 min on Battery Cell I

Excavation

Drilling

Equip the Big Daddy Drill in the tool slot and hold F at a drill site. Ore seams give up resources; sealed walls of basalt open new routes. Some routes only exist once you drill them.

Every material has hardness, durability and a drill effectiveness. Soft rock gives way at once; reinforced relic material fights back.

Logistics

Carry weight

Everything has weight: what you wear and what you haul. Foundry starts with 80 kg of carry and gains 2.5 per level; Relay starts with 45. A backpack adds 20 cells that travel with it and +15 kg. Drop the pack, and everything in it stays with the pack.
