Mining

Ores & minerals

Hold F at a deposit to bore it out. Everything you mine weighs something.

Lumenita

A cold-light crystal that glows on its own and lights the caves it grows in. Rare and brittle; operators pack shards as emergency lamps, and no one has fully explained where the light comes from.

Found inLumenita clusters near The Keystone and in C-3

Redvein Hematite

Blood-red banded iron ore. Dense, reliable, and the reason the first expeditions went down at all.

Found inHematite veins in The Shallows and Excavation C-3

Bog Limonite

Soft ochre iron ore that seeps from wet strata. Crumbles under the drill; the bread-and-butter of every Deepkin forge.

Found inLimonite seams in The Shallows and The Scarp

Lodestone Magnetite

Black, glinting magnetite that pulls at loose tools. Hard to cut, rich in iron, prized by the Foundry.

Found inMagnetite lodes in Excavation C-3 and the Saltpan dig C-7

Currency

Three coins, one stamp. Trading is coming; here is what you will be counting.

Metal Coin

1 Metal

Stamped scrap-metal coin with a square hole: the base unit of DEEPKIN money. Value 1 Metal. Creatures and scavengers drop a few; nothing sells for them yet.

Lumen Coin

100 Metal

The same stamp struck in lumenite-laced alloy; it glows a cold blue. Worth 100 Metal.

Shard

10,000 Metal

A cut crystal shard used for large trades and settlement reserves. Worth 10,000 Metal.
