The world below, in brief

What every operator hears before the lift goes down. Read it next to the map: every place below is on it.

2148 · 2318

The Collapse

Around 2148 the connected world came apart: wars, failing infrastructure, machines that stopped behaving, and a theory nobody can prove — that a general intelligence had truly arrived. Archives vanished, satellites went quiet, and the old Internet broke into the Network: scattered nodes, forgotten servers, systems that still answer. A hundred and seventy years later nobody knows who, or what, is listening on 261.6.66.

Where you start

The Outpost and the shaft

A small fortified mining settlement dug in 60 m down around an old excavation shaft. Newcomer crates, Old Brannock at his rock face, a terminal booth — and the lift car waiting in its well. Every way out passes through The Threshold below; the surface is one shaft, or one adit, away.

Everything has a price

Dustline

The largest trading settlement on the frontier, built from containers and recovered steel around a dead transport hub. Neutral ground where every civilisation buys, sells and hires — and where almost nothing is what it seems. Its unofficial rule: everything has a price; the only question is whether you can afford it.

What hunts below

The Burrowborn

Organic, mineral and wholly of the underground: dark wet hide, mineral plates, a faint green glow, eyes small or gone. They feel your footsteps through the stone long before they see you, and a hurt one calls its kind. Swarm, brute, alpha — and a Deepmaw nobody has met.

People, not monsters

The Castoffs

Scavengers in long coats and balaclavas who live off the wreck yards, the old convoy roads and the first open shafts. Opportunists: they gang up on the lone operator and shout for each other, but they never go deep.

Under the cleft

The Wyrms of the Cinder Deep

Off Frontier Road a stair pit drops 90 m into galleries of an older people, swallowed by basalt. Solitary hunters that share one nest: they rear back before they breathe fire and lick their wounds when left alone. A Greater Dragon keeps the petrified egg. Hunt, take the loot, climb out — they come back.

Deeper still

The excavations

The deeper you dig, the less certain history becomes. C-3 is an old survey: behind its Deep Seal the Dark Stratum (340 m) and the Relic Chamber (520 m), where relics older than the Collapse wait. One day an excavation contract bought in Dustline will call a giant drill to any wild map and carry a crew down floor by floor — as long as there is fuel.

The way down

The starting region

The Outpost → shaft lift → The Threshold → The Shallows → Excavation C-3.

00Settlement

The Outpost

Where every operator arrives. A small fortified mining settlement built around an old excavation shaft.

From the arrival path you already see where you are going: the shaft, ringed with work lights, and the lift gantry right ahead. Workers drill the rock faces, cargo pods shuttle between the yard and the rim, and Old Brannock, the shaft miner, has three things to tell you: “New here?” · “Grab a drill. Grab a weapon.” · “Everything we need comes from below.”

  • Newcomer crates with your starter kit
  • Shaft lift down to The Threshold (G)
  • Adit stairs up to The Scarp
  • Two drill sites to try the drill
  • Control station, power bay, cargo yard

01Cavern

The Threshold

The first cavern below the lift. Quiet, lit and safe enough to learn the basics.

Painted signs teach you to move, equip gear from your loadout, switch on your lantern and dash. The lift takes you back up to the Outpost, the old metro tunnel leads on to The Shallows, and a deep shaft in the floor drops 140 m of stairs straight to Excavation C-3.

  • Move · WASD
  • Loadout · I
  • Lantern · T · Dash · Q
  • Metro · Line 2 to The Shallows
  • Deep shaft · stairs down to C-3
  • Stairs up to The Scarp

02Cavern · hunting ground

The Shallows

A wide, damp cavern around a great stone column: The Keystone. The first real danger, and the first real ore.

Limonite seams and hematite veins wait to be drilled; lumenita crystals glow cold blue near the Keystone. So does the Burrowborn: a Crawler nest sits in front of the Keystone, and at the far east end of the cave the Ravager waits in front of the metro down to C-3. Keep your distance and it charges; get close and it sweeps and slams. When it gets dark, follow the amber lamps back to The Threshold.

  • Crawler nest · respawns every 25–45 s
  • Ravager at the end of the cave · respawns every 90–150 s
  • Limonite, hematite, lumenita
  • Ferrous samples on the ground · Scavengers

03Excavation site

Excavation C-3

An active dig. “Authorised personnel.” The ore face is rich, and someone is paying people to keep it.

Hematite and hard magnetite lodes line the ore face, guarded by Excavation Enforcers. At the far end, a Deep Seal of basalt blocks the way. Drill through it and the Deep Survey Shaft opens: stairs down to FLOOR 2, The Dark Stratum (340 m), and on to FLOOR 3, The Relic Chamber (520 m), whose extraction lift brings you straight back to floor one.

  • Ore face · magnetite and hematite
  • Excavation Enforcers ×2
  • Lift up to The Threshold's deep shaft
  • Deep Seal · basalt, drill to breach
  • Floors 2–3 · 340 m and 520 m · extraction lift

Open sky

The surface

The surface was never completely abandoned. A shaft on The Scarp drops straight to The Threshold.

Surface

The Scarp

Open sky. A wide basin of cracked mud and hoodoo columns, cut by two canyons.

A scavenger camp works the old road. In the middle of the basin stands the SHAFT 3 headframe: its lift drops 120 m straight to The Threshold, and an old adit takes the stairs down to the Outpost. Both canyons lead on to The Saltpan.

Surface

The Saltpan

A salt flat under sandstone cliffs. “The convoy never reached the shaft.”

A wrecked convoy lies along the old road, still holding scrap and a relic fragment. East is a ruined salt works; south, surface dig C-7, a magnetite seam waiting for a drill. Three scavengers think it is all theirs.

Frontier Road

The road east

From The Saltpan the old road runs east to Dustline. A cleft on the way drops into The Cinder Deep.

Surface · road

Frontier Road

East of The Saltpan the old road runs on through a long dry valley of relay pylons and wrecks.

A wrecked convoy still blocks half the road; past it, a waystation keeps water, shade and news for the caravans. In a cleft on the north side, stairs drop into The Cinder Deep. Where the road bends north to Dustline's gate, GATE FUEL sells fuel and gas cans, paid from your bank account.

Trading city

Dustline

The trading city. Neutral ground where every civilisation buys, sells and hires.

Through the gate and the cargo inspection, the Gate Road runs up to the Central Market and the Exchange, whose 261.6.66 terminal holds your bank account and your depot. West is the Caravan Yard, east Scrapper's Row; the Lower Warrens are closed by order of the Exchange.

Hunting ground · −90 m

The Cinder Deep

Under the cleft off Frontier Road: galleries of an older people, swallowed by basalt. The Wyrms hunt here.

Four Dragons hunt the ash hall and the bridge gallery; a Greater Dragon keeps the nest. Come down the stairs, hunt, take the loot and climb back out: they return a minute or two later.
