Lore

The world below, in brief

What every operator hears before the lift goes down. Read it next to the map: every place below is on it.

2148 · 2318

The Collapse

Around 2148 the connected world came apart: wars, failing infrastructure, machines that stopped behaving, and a theory nobody can prove — that a general intelligence had truly arrived. Archives vanished, satellites went quiet, and the old Internet broke into the Network: scattered nodes, forgotten servers, systems that still answer. A hundred and seventy years later nobody knows who, or what, is listening on 261.6.66.

The age below

Four civilisations

What was left of humanity went down. Shelters became cities, and cities became civilisations that agree on almost nothing — except that the surface belongs to the past. Each one sends its own kind of operator back up.

Where you start

The Outpost and the shaft

A small fortified mining settlement dug in 60 m down around an old excavation shaft. Newcomer crates, Old Brannock at his rock face, a terminal booth — and the lift car waiting in its well. The Shaft 3 elevator stops here on its way between the surface and The Threshold below.

Everything has a price

Dustline

The largest trading settlement on the frontier, built from containers and recovered steel around a dead transport hub. Neutral ground where every civilisation buys, sells and hires — and where almost nothing is what it seems. Its unofficial rule: everything has a price; the only question is whether you can afford it.

What hunts below

The Burrowborn

Organic, mineral and wholly of the underground: dark wet hide, mineral plates, a faint green glow, eyes small or gone. They feel your footsteps through the stone long before they see you, and a hurt one calls its kind. Swarm, brute, alpha — and a Deepmaw nobody has met.

People, not monsters

The Castoffs

Scavengers in long coats and balaclavas who live off the wreck yards, the old convoy roads and the first open shafts. Opportunists: they gang up on the lone operator and shout for each other, but they never go deep.

Read more

Under the cleft

The Wyrms of the Cinder Deep

Off Frontier Road a stair pit drops 90 m into galleries of an older people, swallowed by basalt. Solitary hunters that share one nest: they rear back before they breathe fire and lick their wounds when left alone. A Greater Dragon keeps the petrified egg. Hunt, take the loot, climb out — they come back.

Deeper still

The excavations

The deeper you dig, the less certain history becomes. The Deep Survey, under the Shallows, is an old VDI survey: behind its Deep Seal the Dark Stratum (340 m) and the Relic Chamber (520 m), where relics older than the Collapse wait. One day an excavation contract bought in Dustline will call a giant drill to any wild map and carry a crew down floor by floor — as long as there is fuel.

The way down

The starting region

The Outpost → shaft lift → The Threshold → The Shallows → The Deep Survey.

00Settlement

The Outpost

Where every operator arrives. A small fortified mining settlement built around an old excavation shaft.

From the arrival path you already see where you are going: the shaft, ringed with work lights, and the lift gantry right ahead. Workers drill the rock faces, cargo pods shuttle between the yard and the rim, and Old Brannock, the shaft miner, has three things to tell you: “New here?” · “Grab a drill. Grab a weapon.” · “Everything we need comes from below.”

  • Newcomer crates with your starter kit
  • Shaft 3 elevator · up to The Scarp, down to The Threshold (G)
  • Two drill sites to try the drill
  • Control station, power bay, cargo yard

01Cavern

The Threshold

The first cavern below the elevator. Quiet, lit and safe enough to learn the basics.

Painted signs teach you to move, equip gear from your loadout, switch on your lantern and dash. The Shaft 3 elevator takes you back up to the Outpost and on to open sky at The Scarp; the old tunnel leads on to The Shallows.

  • Move · WASD
  • Loadout · I
  • Lantern · T · Dash · Q
  • To The Shallows · tunnel
  • Shaft 3 elevator · up to the Outpost and The Scarp

02Cavern · hunting ground

The Shallows

A wide, damp cavern around a great stone column: The Keystone. The first real danger, and the first real ore.

Limonite seams and hematite veins wait to be drilled; lumenita crystals glow cold blue near the Keystone. So does the Burrowborn: a Crawler nest sits in front of the Keystone, and at the far east end of the cave the Ravager waits in front of the tunnel down to the Deep Survey. Keep your distance and it charges; get close and it sweeps and slams. When it gets dark, follow the amber lamps back to The Threshold.

  • Crawler nest · respawns every 25–45 s
  • Ravager at the end of the cave · respawns every 90–150 s
  • Limonite, hematite, lumenita
  • Ferrous samples on the ground · Scavengers

03Old survey · floors 1–3

The Deep Survey

VDI's old survey workings east of the Shallows. “Authorised personnel.” The ore face is rich, and someone is paying people to keep it.

Hematite and hard magnetite lodes line the ore face, guarded by Excavation Enforcers. At the far end, a Deep Seal of basalt blocks the way. Drill through it and the Deep Survey Shaft opens: stairs down to FLOOR 2, The Dark Stratum (340 m), where two Millennium Bugs tunnel, and on to FLOOR 3, The Relic Chamber (520 m), whose extraction lift brings you straight back to floor one.

  • Ore face · magnetite and hematite
  • Excavation Enforcers ×2
  • Tunnel back to The Shallows
  • Deep Seal · basalt, drill to breach
  • Floors 2–3 · 340 m and 520 m · Millennium Bugs · extraction lift

Open sky

The surface

The surface was never completely abandoned. A shaft on The Scarp drops straight to The Threshold.

Surface

The Scarp

Open sky. A wide basin of cracked mud and hoodoo columns, cut by two canyons.

A scavenger camp works the old road. In the middle of the basin stands the SHAFT 3 headframe: its elevator stops at the Outpost (60 m) and drops on to The Threshold (120 m). Both canyons lead on to The Saltpan.

Surface

The Saltpan

A salt flat under sandstone cliffs. “The convoy never reached the shaft.”

A wrecked convoy lies along the old road, still holding scrap and a relic fragment. East is a ruined salt works; south, surface dig C-7, a magnetite seam waiting for a drill. The Mutants hold the flats' edges: Brood and Acid Spitters on the north rim, Zombine Miners and a Ripper on the west flats, a Tribal Bug in the hoodoo hollow. They keep off the convoy road.

Surface · open-cast pit · 3 levels

The Salt Pit

An old open-cast salt and ore pit in the white flats south of Frontier Road.

Roads come in from three sides: north from The Saltpan, north from Frontier Road and west from Excavation C-3. On the rim, Brine Scorpions hunt round the brine pans in packs, Castoff lookouts and a gunner watch the drop, and Rustbound Miner Units and a Sentry Turret keep the crusher running. The haul road winds down the rust-red terraces, past wrecked trucks and the machine graveyard (Castoff raiders, Crawlers), to the floor 30 m below, where a Millennium Bug tunnels and Crawlers nest. A sealed adit on the floor leads down; what the miners sealed it on, the Pit Wyrm, is not out yet.

Surface · open-cast spiral · 4 levels

Excavation C-3

Vantage Deep Industries' Contract Site 3, south-west of The Scarp: a pit sixty metres deep whose grid never shut down.

Castoff salvage crews camp on the rim, Rustbound Miner Units and a turret keep the substation, Mutant Brood come up at the south lookout. The spiral haul road runs down to the Upper Benches (the abandoned Shift Camp 3 with Zombine Miners, a stalled convoy with Castoff raiders), the Lower Benches (two Millennium Bugs on the borer ground, the first Lumenita faces, a turret on the conveyor head, a Mutant den) and the Rig: Bore 1, guarded by a Haze Skirmisher, a Pelter, a Miner Unit and two Sentry Turrets. Every 150 s the bore spins up and the platform shakes; its Bore Cache stays sealed until the guard is dead, then opens once for each operator. Throw Breaker 3 at the rim substation (hold for 3 s) and the whole pit goes dark for 150 s: the turrets and the bore stop, the machines and Mutants keep hunting. The road east leads to the Salt Pit; the Ruins gate on the west rim is sealed.

Frontier Road

The road east

From The Saltpan the old road runs east to Dustline. A cleft on the way drops into The Cinder Deep.

Surface · road

Frontier Road

East of The Saltpan the old road runs on through a long dry valley of relay pylons and wrecks.

A wrecked convoy still blocks half the road; past it, a waystation keeps water, shade and news for the caravans. In a cleft on the north side, stairs drop into The Cinder Deep; south of the road, past the ambush wrecks, a stair pit under a tarp (KEEP OUT · castoff ground) leads down to the Castoff hideout, and a floodgate in the north-west dam opens on the Sump. Where the road bends north to Dustline's gate, GATE FUEL sells fuel and gas cans, paid from your bank account; off the bay by the gate a board marks the VDI survey clearing, where drillships are called down.

Trading city

Dustline

The trading city. Neutral ground where every civilisation buys, sells and hires.

Through the gate and the cargo inspection, the Gate Road runs up to the Central Market, where a steel fingerpost points the way (Exchange, Scrapper's Row, East Gate, Caravan Yard, Frontier Gate) and every stall trades: Auntie Rhea's empanadas, Casso's rounds, Pell's capsules and battery cells, Sam's lanterns and backpacks, and Lister's contract board. Any trader takes SELL ALL: one trade for all the loot they buy, kit stays. Above the market the Exchange's four 261.6.66 terminals hold your bank account and your depot (with SORT). West is the Caravan Yard: Rask sells Tier-1 weapons, the Rattler, Timber and Headsman chainsaws with the gas cans to run them, and drones, and buys Tier-1 armour; Oksana's bike shop sells motorcycles (2,500 Metal) and cargo bikes (4,500) and cranes a stranded bike back. East are Scrapper's Row, where Nell buys scrap, and the container homes; Vesko the alchemist sells spore and synapse ammunition and recharges an empty cell; Tobe holds the west alley and Marek keeps a stove behind the Warrens. The East Gate opens on the Floodlands, caravans camp on the outskirts, and a VDI agent sells drillship call-outs in the street. The Lower Warrens are still closed by order of the Exchange.

Hunting ground · −90 m

The Cinder Deep

Under the cleft off Frontier Road: galleries of an older people, swallowed by basalt. The Wyrms hunt here.

Four Dragons hunt the ash hall and the bridge gallery; a Greater Dragon keeps the nest. Come down the stairs, hunt, take the loot and climb back out: they return a minute or two later.

Dungeon · 4 floors · −15 to −60 m

The Castoff Hideout

Four floors under Frontier Road east, every one meaner than the last, and the Warlord at the bottom.

The Yard, the Works, the Barracks and the Warlord's Hall: lookouts and raiders first, then gunners, troopers and a vanguard. Every floor starts on a lit landing no Castoff can see. The Warlord holds the hall from a broken dais beside his truck; his strongbox opens once for each operator, and the contract board in Dustline pays for his head. A service lift takes you back up to the road.

Surface · wetland

The Sump

A basin north of the road that turns from cracked mud to peat and reeds to standing water.

Scavengers keep a lean-to on the dry west side; duckboards carry the old road over the peat past a sluice weir. In the north-east a drowned VDI pump station sinks into the water, and beside its open bunker hatch a railed stair pit (PUMP WORKS 7) goes down. Two War Drones patrol the water behind it.

Dungeon · 4 floors · −30 to −120 m

The Pump Works

Under the Sump, VDI's machines still receive instructions. Nobody sends them.

The Intake Hall, the Relay Gallery, the Assembly Line and the Switchyard: Miner Units on patrol, War Drones, Sentry Turrets, Haze Skirmishers and a Pelter, with spike traps on every floor. In the Switchyard the grid blacks out every 30 seconds: lights, turrets and traps go dark while the Chopper keeps hunting, so bring a lantern. Its cache holds a telemetry log that is still being written.

Surface · east of Dustline

The Floodlands

Through Dustline's East Gate, a gravel causeway runs between standing water.

North, a half-sunk church on its island, reached by boardwalks, and the remains of a bunker; south, a marsh with a drowned van. Two Sluice Maws, rooted Rustbound flood intakes, guard the drowned quay and the south marsh: keep 12 m off their tendril. East are the drowned quay with its dock crane and the cellar ruin, where a railed stair pit drops into the Drowned Cellar: a flooded hall with brick piers, four Crawlers and a Ravager in the back room. Two more gateways stand SEALED, not yet surveyed.

Camp outside the walls

Dustline outskirts

Travellers who will not pay to sleep inside camp outside the walls.

A bus-house, a caravan and an RV round the family fire: Mama Zuri buys ore, the only ore buyer in Dustline, and sells rounds; Odo Ferrant, the tinker, sells gas cans and Servo Cores. Round the wall's corner stand the west tents and the guards' tents by the road.
