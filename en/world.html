The way down

The starting region

00Settlement

The Outpost

Where every operator arrives. A small fortified mining settlement built around an old excavation shaft.

From the arrival path you already see where you are going: the shaft, ringed with work lights, and the lift gantry right ahead. Workers drill the rock faces, cargo pods shuttle between the yard and the rim, and Old Brannock, the shaft miner, has three things to tell you: “New here?” · “Grab a drill. Grab a weapon.” · “Everything we need comes from below.”

  • Newcomer crates with your starter kit
  • Shaft lift down to The Threshold (G)
  • Two drill sites to try the drill
  • Control station, power bay, cargo yard

01Cavern

The Threshold

The first cavern below the lift. Quiet, lit and safe enough to learn the basics.

Painted signs teach you to move, equip gear from your loadout, switch on your lantern and dash. Lifts on the walls take you back up to the Outpost, and the passages lead on to The Shallows.

  • Move · WASD
  • Loadout · I
  • Lantern · T · Dash · Q
  • Passage up from The Scarp

02Cavern · hunting ground

The Shallows

A wide, damp cavern around a great stone column: The Keystone. The first real danger, and the first real ore.

Limonite seams and hematite veins wait to be drilled; lumenita crystals glow cold blue near the Keystone. So does the Burrowborn: a Crawler nest guards the way down, and a Ravager holds the wide chamber beside it. When it gets dark, follow the amber lamps back to The Threshold.

  • Crawler nest · respawns every 25–45 s
  • Ravager den · respawns every 90–150 s
  • Limonite, hematite, lumenita
  • Ferrous samples on the ground · Scavengers

03Excavation site

Excavation C-3

An active dig. “Authorised personnel.” The ore face is rich, and someone is paying people to keep it.

Hematite and hard magnetite lodes line the ore face, guarded by Excavation Enforcers. At the far end, a Deep Seal of basalt blocks the way. Drill through it and you find the Deep Survey Shaft, and the question of how far down the old surveys really went.

  • Ore face · magnetite and hematite
  • Excavation Enforcers ×2
  • Deep Seal · basalt, drill to breach
  • Discovery: Deep Survey Shaft

Open sky

The surface

The surface was never completely abandoned. A shaft on The Scarp drops straight to The Threshold.

Surface

The Scarp

Open sky. A wide basin of cracked mud and hoodoo columns, cut by two canyons.

A scavenger camp works the old road. In the middle of the basin, an excavation crater and a Big Daddy rig: the shaft inside it drops straight to The Threshold. Both canyons lead on to The Saltpan.

Surface

The Saltpan

A salt flat under sandstone cliffs. “The convoy never reached the shaft.”

A wrecked convoy lies along the old road, still holding scrap and a relic fragment. East is a ruined salt works; south, surface dig C-7, a magnetite seam waiting for a drill. Three scavengers think it is all theirs.
