Weapons

Any class can carry any weapon; the class sets how hard it hits and how fast it trains. Hit and DPS are for the weapon's home class (the one that trains its skill fastest) at skill 1 and level 1, sustained, with its own ammunition. Open a weapon for every class, or try the calculator below.

How the numbers work

hit = base × class coefficient × attribute bonus × skill & level bonus × ammo × (1 + trait %)

Opening is the first hit; sustained is the steady state (Heat and Suppression at their cap, Range at 6 m+, Overcharge with the cell above half). Ambush counts on half the hits; the Drift adds Trailblazer +8% on the same target; spore poison is added per second. Crush only applies to elites and bosses, so it is left out of DPS.

Every weapon trains its skill at the same rate per second of fighting: 10 XP/s × class affinity. Tier 2 and up also ask for your skill: T2 10, T3 20, T4 35, T5 50, T6 70.

  • BladesSwords, axes and chainsaws. Heat: chained hits within 3 s deal +5 / +10 / +15% damage (a running chainsaw starts at +15%).
  • BluntHammers and maces. Stagger: a blow delays the enemy's next attack. Crush: extra damage against elites and bosses.
  • Short BladesKnives and daggers. Ambush: +30% damage after moving 2 m since your last hit; finishing blows on wounded humanoids.
  • FirearmsShotguns, carbines and rifles. Suppression: each consecutive hit on the same target within 2 s adds +5% damage, up to +15%.
  • PrecisionPrecision rifles. +15% damage on targets 6 m or more away. Later: weak points.
  • SporesSpore projectors. Spore cartridges poison what they hit; with one in hand, Bloom poisons enemies instead of healing.
  • VitalismLiving weapons that fire synapse darts: they wound enemies and heal the operators they strike. Later: stronger healing.
  • TechEnergy weapons and field electronics. Each shot draws from the worn cell. Overcharge: +15% damage while the cell is above half.
