01
The expedition
Four ideas hold the whole game together.

  
Explore
Connected caverns, open surface and sealed ruins. Every route you find is knowledge you keep.
Excavate
Drilling is how you discover the world: it opens passages, pulls ore from the rock and reveals what was buried.
Equip
Thirteen gear slots, no class locks. Weapon, armor, battery and light are your build.
Cooperate
Four civilisations, four roles. A crew that covers each other goes deeper and comes back.

  
  1. 01
    Prepare
    Pick your gear, charge your battery, pack ammunition.
  2. 02
    Descend
    Ride the shaft lift or find a way down from the surface.
  3. 03
    Fight & drill
    Clear threats, bore out ore, breach sealed walls.
  4. 04
    Decide
    Your light and your health are finite. Push deeper, or turn back?
  5. 05
    Return
    Bring back what you secured and build your next expedition.
