Game mechanics

Death

What dying costs, where your things end up and where you wake up.

When you die

  1. Experience: you lose 10% of your total XP — it can cost a level — never below level 1.
  2. Weapon skills: each skill loses 5% of its XP (it can cost a skill level).
  3. Backpack: your worn Backpack always drops, with everything in it.
  4. Extra drops: 50% chance of one more item, then 25% of a second (so 12.5% of deaths drop two).
  5. Extra drops are picked from what you wear in: Head, Chest, Arms, Legs, Boots, Primary weapon, Off hand, Light, Modification 1, Modification 2, and from your pockets.
  6. Never dropped: Tool, Battery. They are what gets you back on your feet.
  7. Gear you no longer qualify for after losing a level comes off into a free pocket (or the remains).
  8. Everything dropped goes into one Operator Remains (12 cells) at the spot where you fell. Anyone can loot it; it crumbles after 30 minutes.
  9. Respawn: 4 s later you are back at full health at your bound respawn hub.
