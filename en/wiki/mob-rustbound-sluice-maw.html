Lore

When the east drowned, VDI's floodworks bolted Sluice Maws along the quays: an intake head on a hydraulic stalk that clamps whatever drifts into the channel - timber, drums, the odd body - and feeds it to the pumps so nothing jams them. The pumps stopped long ago; the maws never got the instruction. Weed and rust grew over the plating, and they still clear the water of anything that moves.

Behaviour

ROOT LASH SNAP SPRAY THRASH

  • Notices you within 12.5 m (it can sense up to 14 m) and remembers your last position for 4 s after losing you.
  • Rooted: it never leaves its spot. Out of its reach it can only watch you.
  • It fights from 0–12 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Snap — 3.4 m reach in a 100° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Tendril Lash — A glowing strip 1.4 m wide, from 3.5 to 12 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Sap Spray — A 70° fan, 6.5 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Resistance.
  • Thrash — It rears up and a 3.4 m ring around it glows: step out within 1.1 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • It never moves. Past its tendril's reach (about 12 m) it can only watch you.
  • A blue line along the water is its tendril: it lashes out and snares whoever it catches (45 % speed for 2.5 s). Step off the line.
  • Close in front, its jaws gape over a red arc: step to its side before they snap shut.
  • A green fan: it spits sap from its throat (acid; Resistance helps).
  • Hug its stalk and it thrashes: a red ring fills all around it. Step out.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
