Lore

A VDI shift that went down a shaft and came back up. The helmet lamp still works; the man under it does not. It never lets go of the pick.

Behaviour

SHAMBLE GRAB SWING FOLLOW

  • Notices you within 13.5 m (it can sense up to 16.5 m) and remembers your last position for 9 s after losing you.
  • Predator: once it has your scent it follows you far, up to 28 m from home.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 8 m.
  • It fights from 0–1.6 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Grab — 1.9 m reach in a 80° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Pick Swing — It rears up and the ground glows in a 1.8 m ring: step out within 1 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • Slow, but it never stops following: it remembers where you went.
  • It reaches out before it grabs — the red arc — and the grab hurts. Step back out of reach.
  • The pick comes down in a red ring: don't stand in it.
