Game mechanics

Party

Operators group up from the field: right-click another operator to follow them or invite them to a party.

Playing it

  • A quick right-click on another operator in your zone opens a menu: FOLLOW and INVITE TO PARTY (greyed with the reason when you cannot invite).
  • Follow walks you toward them and stops 2.2 m short; any movement key ends it. The server checks that movement like any other, so following gives no advantage.
  • An invite shows a toast with ACCEPT / DECLINE and a bar that drains over 60 s. A newer invite replaces an older one.
  • The party panel (left, under your plate) lists every member: level, callsign, the zone they stand in, health and battery percentage. The leader toggles SHARED XP and can PROMOTE or KICK; anyone can LEAVE.

Operators have no chosen names yet: the party shows a callsign like RELAY·001 (class and number).

Rules

  • Up to 4 members. Invite only an online operator in your zone within 40 m who is not in a party; if you are in a party you must lead it.
  • The party is created on the first accept, with the inviter as leader. A leaving leader passes the lead to the longest-standing member; a party left with one member disbands.
  • Kick, promote and the shared-XP switch are for the leader only. Shared XP survives a change of leader.
  • Parties live only while the server runs: a disconnect is a leave and nothing is saved.
  • Members see each other's zone, level, health and battery percentage wherever they stand, never positions, cargo or exact energy.
  • A scan drone's radar is shared with the whole party, wherever each member stands.

Shared experience

With SHARED XP on, the kill XP the members earned is pooled and split equally among the members in the kill's zone within 30 m of the creature, whether or not they hit it, as long as the lowest level among them is at least 2/3 of the highest. Otherwise (or with fewer than two in range) each keeps their own damage share. Players outside the party are untouched.

Example

A 300 XP kill, three members in range, levels 12, 15 and 17:

12 ≥ 2/3 × 17 = 11.3: each gets 100 XP.

With levels 9 and 17: 9 < 2/3 × 17 = 11.3, so the panel shows LV GAP and each keeps their own share.
