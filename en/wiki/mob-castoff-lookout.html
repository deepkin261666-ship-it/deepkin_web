Lore

Young, sharp-eyed, too old for the Outpost's school and too young for its payroll. The hideout pays its lookouts in rounds.

Behaviour

WATCH SHOUT AIM BACK OFF

  • Notices you within 10 m (it can sense up to 12 m) and remembers your last position for 4 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 11 m.
  • At 30% health it runs for 2.5 s — and comes back.
  • It fights from 4.5–7.5 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Aimed Shot — A glowing strip 0.7 m wide, from 3 to 11 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • She sees you first and shouts: every Castoff within earshot comes running.
  • She keeps her distance and plinks; before a real shot she shoulders the rifle and a thin line marks where it goes — step off it.
  • Hurt, she backs away to shoot again: close the gap or use cover.
