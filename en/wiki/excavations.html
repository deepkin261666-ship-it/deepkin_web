Excavations (VDI)

Vantage Deep Industries will drop a drillship anywhere open you call it and bore floors for you: generated per call-out, not fixed zones.

Playing it

  1. Get an Expedition Service: Vdi Field Unit sells it for 10,000 Metal, and his contract Where The World Ends gives the first one free.
  2. Use it (double-click, its right-click menu or a hotkey): the ship lands 13 m ahead of you. Not in settlements, underground, on roads, near passages, arrivals or fuel stations, at a map's edge, within 36 m of another ship, or on ground that is not open and clear. The service is spent; you are the Owner; one open excavation per owner.
  3. For 6 s an amber ring marks the site while the ship falls; anyone under it is pushed to the edge. Then it stays: a huge cylinder with one door and a floor display.
  4. Roster members press E at the door: inside is the first-person control room (no combat). E at the terminal opens the VDI Excavation software; E at the door steps out on the floor the ship is on.
  5. The owner closes it (close, then close confirm): everyone is put back on the surface, the floors and everything in them are removed and the ground is free again.

Reserves and floors

The ship arrives with 40 energy and 10 L of fuel (max 600 / 120). Each leg down to depth d costs 25·d energy and 6·d L, so the costs add up as you go deeper; going up is free. A trip locks the door for 5 s. The ship will not leave a floor while a member still stands on it. The number of floors is rolled per excavation from the map's danger: 2–3 in the starting areas, up to 15.

Rules

Open excavations survive a server restart (the ship stands again on the surface, reserves and roster kept); floors are regenerated from their seed with fresh creatures and resources. Creatures, drops and remains below are lost on a restart or a close.

Not to be confused with The Deep Survey: that is an old, fixed VDI survey below the Shallows, reached from the Outpost through the Threshold and the Shallows. The Deep Survey

Nor with Excavation C-3, VDI's Contract Site 3: the spiral pit in the south-west of the map, a planned fixed site (coming soon). Excavation C-3
