Lore

A deserter from a convoy guard who walked off with his gas mask and his habits. Nobody has seen his face since.

Behaviour

HOLD BURST SUPPRESS FALL BACK

  • Notices you within 12 m (it can sense up to 13 m) and remembers your last position for 5 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 10 m.
  • It fights from 6–9 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Burst — A glowing strip 0.8 m wide, from 3 to 13 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Suppressing Fire — A 32° fan, 8 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Afterwards it backs off for 1.2 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • He stays at rifle range. A burst follows a marked line — step off it or put a wall between you.
  • Suppressing fire sprays a narrow fan: get behind cover or out to its side, then he falls back to reload.
  • Close the distance and he is slow to turn.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
