Lore

More machine than man below the collarbones. He came up out of the deep shafts with a crate of armour nobody in the Frontier can make, and the Castoffs have followed him since. He pays in rounds, in water and in whatever an operator drops.

Behaviour

LANCE CHARGE STOMP SWEEP CALL THE GUARD

  • Notices you within 13 m (it can sense up to 16 m) and remembers your last position for 10 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 20 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 22 m.
  • It fights from 0–2 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Rail Lance — A glowing strip 1.3 m wide, from 4.5 to 16 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Overclock Charge — A red lane 1.8 m wide lights up in front of it when you are 4–10 m away. It throws its weight along the locked facing, stops at the first wall or the first operator in the lane, and that operator takes the hit: step off the lane sideways. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Shock Stomp — It rears up and a 3.4 m ring around it glows: step out within 1.1 s. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Arc Sweep — A 140° fan, 4.5 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • The boss of the hideout and its fourth floor. Hit him and every Castoff in the room comes for you.
  • At range he fires a rail lance down a long marked line; from a few metres he charges along a red lane.
  • Close in he sweeps a wide arc and stomps a red ring around himself: the ring is the cue to step out, the arc to get behind him.
  • He never heals. Keep the pressure on between his blows — each one leaves him open for a second.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 20 m from home.
