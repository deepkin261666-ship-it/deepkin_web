Game mechanics

Skills & powers

Skills belong to weapon families, not classes. Every landed hit trains the family of the weapon that dealt it.

The nine skillsAffinity by classSkill levelsClass powers & DashClass passivesSee also

The nine skills

10 skill XP per hit that lands, 8 per hit taken with a shield up, times your class affinity (untrained families learn at ×0.5). Each skill level adds 1% damage with that family, up to level 100.

Blades Blades

Swords and axes. Chained hits build Heat: +2, +4, +6 damage within 3 s.

Weapons: Breaker Axe, Scrapblade · trains fastest: FOUNDRY

Blunt Blunt

Hammers and maces. Slow, heavy blows that hit hardest per swing.

Later: staggering and breaking armour and rock.

Weapons: Breaker Hammer, Breaker Mace · trains fastest: FOUNDRY

Short Blades Short Blades

Knives and daggers. Ambush: +4 damage after moving 2 m since your last hit.

Later: silent takedowns.

Weapons: Relay Knife · trains fastest: RELAY

Firearms Firearms

Shotguns, carbines and rifles. Steady fire at mid range.

Weapons: Frontier Shotgun, Scout Rifle, Rivet Caster · trains fastest: DRIFT

Precision Precision

Precision rifles. +3 damage on targets 6 m or more away.

Later: weak points.

Weapons: Longshot · trains fastest: DRIFT

Spores Spores

Spore projectors. With one in hand, Bloom poisons enemies instead of healing.

Later: fungal growth and area denial.

Weapons: Spore Projector · trains fastest: VERDANT

Vitalism Vitalism

Living weapons that grow with their bearer.

Later: stronger healing and tending the living.

Weapons: Synapse Carbine · trains fastest: VERDANT

Tech Tech

Energy weapons and field electronics.

Later: hacking terminals, doors, turrets and drones.

Weapons: Relay Carbine · trains fastest: RELAY

Shielding Shielding

Shields. Trains when you are hit with a shield up; 8% less damage, up to 40%.

Later: active block and parry.

Weapons: Sentinel Shield · trains fastest: FOUNDRY
