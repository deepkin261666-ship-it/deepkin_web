Lore

A light combat mech from a VDI security contract. Plasma caster on one arm, a rotary cutter on the other. Provisional lore.

Behaviour

STRIDE PLASMA SWEEP HOLD THE LINE

  • Notices you within 10 m (it can sense up to 13 m) and remembers your last position for 6 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 18 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 2–7 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Plasma Caster — A glowing strip 1.2 m wide, from 3 to 12 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Rotary Cutter — A 110° fan, 4 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • At range it charges its plasma caster along a long glowing line: sidestep it, or break line of sight.
  • Close in, the rotary cutter sweeps a wide arc in front of it.
  • It does not leave its hall.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 18 m from home.
