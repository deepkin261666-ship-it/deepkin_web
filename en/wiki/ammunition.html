Game mechanics

Ammunition & energy weapons

Every ranged weapon fires only its own ammunition family; energy weapons fire from your battery cell instead.

How it worksThe familiesSpores: poisonSynapse: healing dartsEnergy weaponsShort Blades: executionsSee also

How it works

  • The weapon loads the first stack of its family in your top-level cargo; other families are skipped (a Spore Projector never fires Field Rounds).
  • The stronger kind of a family (Hardened, Virulent, Surge) is drawn heavier for everyone.
  • The HUD ammo panel names the family and the loaded kind (ROUNDS · FIELD, SPORES · VIRULENT, SYNAPSE · SURGE); for an energy weapon it reads CELL · cost per shot.
  • Ammunition multipliers scale the weapon hit; poison ticks and heals are flat (no class or skill scaling).

Spores: poison

A hit poisons the creature: flat ticks once a second, credited to the shooter (kill XP and aggro). Hitting it again keeps the tick clock and takes the stronger poison and the later end: poison does not stack. It only affects creatures.

Synapse: healing darts

Aimed at (or clicked on) another operator, a dart heals instead of damaging: there is no friendly fire. The nearest operator on the line of fire wins over an enemy farther along it. Heals cap at full health and never revive; a heal trains Vitalism. A dart at a full-health ally is still spent.

Energy weapons

Tech ranged weapons load no rounds: every shot draws 1.5 energy from your worn battery cell. Below one shot's cost the trigger is refused (NO CHARGE). A bare 100-energy cell holds 66 shots; hits train Tech. The energy readout counts sustained fire as a drain.

Short Blades: executions

Any Short Blades hit (Twin Blades, Relay Knife) on a humanoid creature at or below 25% of its health finishes it: the hit deals at least its remaining health and plays an execution. The server decides it; dragons, Burrowborn and other non-humanoids are never executed.
