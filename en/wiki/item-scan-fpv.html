Description

A quick racing-frame quad refitted with a survey sensor. Launch it and stand still at its laptop: you fly it with your movement keys and the camera follows it up to 40 m away, while its radar (24 m around it) marks terrain, ore and hostiles for you and your party. Moving or getting hit drops the link and it flies home. Draws 0.45 energy a second from your worn cell, two minutes a flight. Needs Tech 3. Relay Nine stock; Rask sells it in Dustline.
