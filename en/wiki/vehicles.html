Vehicles

The motorcycle is the first step of mobility: on foot, then your own machine. Oksana's bike shop in Dustline sells a second one and a cargo bike with a hold, and cranes a stranded bike home.

How it works

  • Every character owns one motorcycle: it is parked next to you the first time you enter the world (at the Outpost landing).
  • G within 2.5 m of your bike gets on; WASD steers and throttles; G again gets off.
  • Fuel is spent by distance actually travelled (0.05 per metre: 20 m per unit), never by time. Standing still costs nothing.
  • At 20% the gauge turns amber (LOW FUEL); at zero the bike coasts to a stop and stays there until you bring fuel.
  • While riding you cannot attack, dash, use powers or drill.
  • Only its owner can ride it, and it is only in the world while its owner is online.

The bike shop

MOTO · DUSTLINE is a small garage against the Caravan Yard's north fence. Oksana sells the bikes and runs the crane; sold and craned bikes park on the painted bay in front (3 spots).

  • Buying: stand within reach of the dealer, pay in carried coins (not the bank), own fewer than 4 vehicles and leave a free spot in the bay. The new bike arrives with its starting fuel, a full frame and an empty hold.
  • Crane: hauls one of your stranded bikes of that kind (empty tank or wrecked frame, not ridden) from anywhere to the bay for 30% of its list price. It does not refuel: buy a can at the gate station.
  • No sale back to the shop; the crane picks the first stranded bike of the kind.

Prices and numbers are provisional balance and may change.

The cargo motorcycle's hold

The Cargo Motorcycle carries a hold of 20 cells and up to 60 kg. Click the bike (or press I beside it) to open it like a ground bag: only its owner, within 4 m or riding it, flat items only (no bags). The hold is saved with the bike.

Refuelling

Refuel at a gas station's pump computer (paid from the bank) or pour a gas can into the tank anywhere.

  • Frontier Road: GATE FUEL · 2 Metal/L · Cargo Motorcycle ≈ 240 Metal · Motorcycle ≈ 200 Metal
  • Gas Can: 20 L ≈ 333.333 m (Cargo Motorcycle) · 400 m (Motorcycle)

Riding through passages

Passages: you can ride through tunnels, elevators, lifts and gates (G near one: RIDE THROUGH) and the bike comes with you. Stairs are on foot only: G parks the bike at their head (GET OFF · STAIRS).
