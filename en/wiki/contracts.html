Game mechanics

Contracts

A contract is a job with a crypto payment: the employer pays on a Contract Drive that you cash at any bank terminal.

How it worksContractsDrivesSee also

How it works

  1. Take the paper from the giver, do the job, then come back: the last step hands you the drive.
  2. Cash it at the BANK app of any 261.6.66 terminal: type insert drive (or click the hint). One drive is taken from your cargo and its value is credited to your balance.
  3. A drive in a spare bag does not count, and a drive that would push your balance past its limit stays with you.
  4. Until then it is an ordinary item: it can be dropped, picked up by anyone, stored in the depot or lost when you die. A drive pays whoever slots it.
bank@261.6.66:~$ insert drive
