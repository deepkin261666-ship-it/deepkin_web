Lore

Its forearms grew into glowing bone blades. It leaps at whatever moves on the flats and tears at it.

Behaviour

PROWL LEAP REND FOLLOW

  • Notices you within 13.5 m (it can sense up to 16.5 m) and remembers your last position for 8 s after losing you.
  • Predator: once it has your scent it follows you far, up to 28 m from home.
  • It fights from 0–1.7 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Leap — A red lane 1.6 m wide lights up in front of it when you are 3.5–7 m away. It throws its weight along the locked facing, stops at the first wall or the first operator in the lane, and that operator takes the hit: step off the lane sideways. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Rend — A 120° fan, 2.6 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • Keep your distance and it leaps along a red lane: step off it.
  • Close in, both blades rake across a wide red arc in front of it.
  • It hunts: once it has you it follows far past its flats.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
