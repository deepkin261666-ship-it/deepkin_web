Game mechanics

Bank & currency

DEEPKIN money: creatures drop Metal Coins; the bank keeps your balance safe and pays out in Metal, Lumen Coins and Shards.

The bank

Every character has one bank account, a whole number of Metal (up to 1,000,000,000,000). Open BANK on any 261.6.66 terminal (The Outpost, Dustline) after connect 261.6.66. Banked Metal is safe from death and the same at every terminal.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
balanceShows your balance.
deposit allDeposits every coin in your pouch and backpack (coins inside a spare bag do not count).
deposit <n>Deposits that many Metal, smallest coins first; if it has to break a coin it pays the change back in the largest coins.
withdraw allPays out everything that fits in your cargo, largest coins first; the rest stays in the account.
withdraw <n>Pays out that many Metal, in the largest coins that fit your free cells and carry weight.
transfer <n> to <name>Sends Metal to another operator by name, online or offline.
help · clear · exithelp · clear · exit (or Esc)

Amounts are whole Metal and may be written 1,500, 1_500 or 250m. Decimals, zero and negatives are refused.

Example

guest@outpost-7:~$ connect 261.6.66
…
bank@261.6.66:~$ deposit all
>> DEPOSITED 237 METAL. BALANCE 1,530 METAL
bank@261.6.66:~$ withdraw all
>> PAID OUT 10,000 METAL OF 10,230 METAL — YOUR CARGO IS FULL
bank@261.6.66:~$ transfer 50 to nobody
ERR NO OPERATOR BY THAT NAME …

Good to know

  • A deposit and the coins it takes are saved together: if the save fails, nothing moves.
  • Withdrawals never overflow your cargo: if not everything fits you get what fits (PARTIAL) and the rest stays banked.
  • Transfers debit you first and credit the receiver second; if that fails you are re-credited, so Metal is never lost or created.
  • Only the bank pays out Lumen Coins and Shards today; creatures drop Metal Coins.

Where coins go

Fuel and gas cans at the gas station, paid from your bank balance. A coin stack you carry can still drop into your remains when you die; banked Metal never does.
