Lore

The heavy four-legged wyrm that keeps the nest. It does not chase; it holds its ground and burns everything in front of it.

In the game

Behaviour

GUARD MAUL BREATHE SCORCH HOLD LICK WOUNDS

  • Notices you within 8 m (it can sense up to 13 m) and remembers your last position for 4 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 14 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 0–3 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Lick Wounds — Restores 8% of its health (45 HP) when below 55%, unhit for 5 s and away from its target. Any hit or a stun breaks the channel.
  • Firestorm Breath — A 96° fan, 8.5 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Resistance.
  • Crushing Bite — 3.4 m reach in a 120° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Fire Bolt — A glowing strip 1.6 m wide, from 5 to 14 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Resistance.

How to fight it

  • It guards the nest and will not follow you far: pull it to the edge of its ground and it turns back.
  • Its breath covers a wide fan in front of it and hits hard: when it rears back, get to its flank or behind a pillar.
  • From range it hurls a heavy fire bolt along a glowing line — step off the line.
  • Slow, tough and patient: it heals if you give it room, so keep a steady pressure on it.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
  • Keep hitting it: the heal only starts when it is left alone, and any hit breaks it.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 14 m from home.
