Lore

A Burrowborn that came up the Salt Pit's old adit and stayed in the light: its plates have crusted white with salt. It feels footsteps through the flats long before it sees you, and it never hunts alone.

Behaviour

SKITTER SPRAY STING CALL

  • Notices you within 8 m (it can sense up to 11 m) and remembers your last position for 5 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 9 m.
  • It fights from 0–1.4 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Salt Spray — A 64° fan, 4 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Tail Sting — 1.9 m reach in a 80° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • Before it sprays, a blue fan lights up in front of it: step out of it, or the salt slows you for two seconds.
  • Close in, its tail curls before it stings: a short red arc.
  • Hit one and the rest of the pack comes running.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
