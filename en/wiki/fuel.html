West of the road where it bends north to the gate: a forecourt with two numbered pumps, a canopy with a red fascia, the pump computer (PAY HERE · NET 261.6.66) and the SELF SERV GAS pole sign.

Open on the world map

Using a pump

  1. Park your own motorcycle within 3.6 m of a pump and get off (G).
  2. Stand in front of the pump computer (2.5 m, screen side) and press E: PUMP COMPUTER · GATE FUEL. No login needed.
  3. The screen shows the price, your bank balance, your tank (with the cost to fill it) and the first gas can you carry.
  4. 1 FILL TANK · 2 FILL GAS CAN · 3 BUY GAS CAN (25 Metal) · Esc to leave. Keys F, C and B work too.

Everything is paid from your bank balance, never from the coins you carry. A fill buys as many litres as your balance pays for, up to what the tank or can holds, rounded up to whole Metal.

The gas can

Gas Can holds 20 L and weighs 4 kg; it never stacks. Bought empty at a station, filled at the pump computer, and carried anywhere: right-click it (REFUEL VEHICLE) or put it on a hotkey to pour it into your bike within mount reach. Its cell shows the litres it holds.

Tip: a full can is 333.333 m of riding. Keep one in the backpack before taking the bike down the road or into the tunnels.
