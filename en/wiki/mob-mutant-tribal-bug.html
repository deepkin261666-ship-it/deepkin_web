Lore

Something that walks upright, wears feathers and scrap, and carries a club made of another creature's jaw. The other mutants keep away from its hollow.

Behaviour

GUARD CHARGE SWEEP CRUSH

  • Notices you within 13.5 m (it can sense up to 16.5 m) and remembers your last position for 8 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 30 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 0–2 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Bug Charge — A red lane 2 m wide lights up in front of it when you are 4–9 m away. It throws its weight along the locked facing, stops at the first wall or the first operator in the lane, and that operator takes the hit: step off the lane sideways. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Club Sweep — A 150° fan, 3 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Mandible Crush — It rears up and the ground glows in a 2.2 m ring: step out within 1.1 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • The strongest thing on the salt flats. It charges along a wide red lane — get out of it, never back away in a straight line.
  • Close in, the club sweeps a wide red arc; before the crush it raises the club and the ground glows red.
  • It will follow you a long way out of its hollow, but not onto the road.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 30 m from home.
