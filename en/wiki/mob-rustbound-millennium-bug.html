Lore

A VDI survey borer: a segmented drill-worm that tunnels ahead of the dig crews and lights its own way with red lamps. Its work clock rolled over in the year two thousand and read the date as nineteen hundred, so every survey on its list is a century overdue and it will not stop for anything until they are done. The miners call them millennium bugs. Anything warm that stands on its survey line is rock to be cut.

Behaviour

TUNNEL ERUPT SHEAR SURGE DIG AGAIN

  • Notices you within 9 m (it can sense up to 14 m) and remembers your last position for 6 s after losing you.
  • Burrowing: it dives and resurfaces at home.
  • It fights from 0–2 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Tunnel Strike — From 4.5 m away it dives (it takes almost no damage while under) and a 2.4 m red ring fills under the operator it chases, locked when it dives; after 1.6 s it erupts there. Keep moving when the ground cracks under you, then hit it when it comes up. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Shear Jaws — 2.4 m reach in a 90° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Afterwards it backs off for 1.2 s. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Fault Surge — It rears up and a 3 m ring around it glows: step out within 0.9 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • Step back from it and it digs: a red ring cracks open under your feet. Move before it fills; it comes up there.
  • While it is under the rock your blows barely reach it: hit it when it comes up.
  • Close in, its head rears up and back before the shear jaws snap: a short red arc in front of it. Then it backs off to dig again.
  • When its lamps stutter and a blue ring fills around it, step out: the surge jams your suit for a moment.
