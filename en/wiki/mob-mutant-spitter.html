Lore

Its head split open around a second mouth that spits what its stomach makes of the salt. Whatever it hits smokes.

Behaviour

KEEP AWAY SPIT SPRAY BACK OFF

  • Notices you within 14 m (it can sense up to 17 m) and remembers your last position for 8 s after losing you.
  • Intelligent: it stops and waits instead of following blindly.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 10 m.
  • It fights from 5–9 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Acid Spray — A 70° fan, 4 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Afterwards it backs off for 1.5 s. Reduced by your Resistance.
  • Acid Spit — A glowing strip 0.9 m wide, from 3 to 11 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Resistance.

How to fight it

  • It keeps its distance and spits acid along a green line: the line glows before the spit — sidestep it or put rock between you.
  • Get close and it sprays a green fan of acid, then backs off to spit again.
  • Acid is biological damage: Resistance protects, Armour does not.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
