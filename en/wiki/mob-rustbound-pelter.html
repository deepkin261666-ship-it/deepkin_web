Lore

An artillery walker that lobs shells at whatever its targeting array marks. Its shells land where the circles glow. Provisional lore.

Behaviour

PLANT SHELL STOMP

  • Notices you within 12 m (it can sense up to 15 m) and remembers your last position for 6 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 16 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 6–12 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Salvo — Red circles of 2 m mark where the shells land, on a fixed pattern around the facing it locks at the start; they all hit at once after 1.6 s. Stand between the circles or close in under 4 m. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Stomp — It rears up and the ground glows in a 2.6 m ring: step out within 0.9 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • From range it plants its legs and lobs a salvo: red circles walk out toward you, and the shells land where they glow. Leave the circles.
  • Up close it stomps a ring around itself.
  • Slow: get inside its range and stay mobile.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 16 m from home.
