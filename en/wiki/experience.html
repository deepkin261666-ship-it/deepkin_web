The curve

total_xp(L) = 50/3 · (L³ − 6L² + 17L − 12)

There is no level cap and every level costs more than the one before (Tibia's curve). Level 2 takes 100 XP. The only bound is level 65,535, a storage limit nobody reaches.

What a level gives you

Levels never raise health, armor or attributes. Each level adds 0.6% to all your damage and raises your carry cap:
