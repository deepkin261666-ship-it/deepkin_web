How it works

  1. Walk up to a booth (within 2.5 m, on the screen side) and press E.
  2. At the prompt, type connect 261.6.66 exactly. help lists commands, exit or Esc closes.
  3. Menu: DEPOT, BANK, MARKET, CONTRACTS, MAIL / NEWS (market, contracts and mail are offline for now), LOGOUT.
  4. The depot holds 40 cells. Double-click, Enter or T moves a stack between your cargo and the depot; drag to a specific cell. [ SORT ] over either side (or O for the side under the cursor) merges partial stacks and orders that side: coins, weapons, armour, ammunition, batteries and fuel, food, drones, materials, then logs. Bags keep their cell. The same SORT sits over the CARGO grid of your loadout screen.
  5. One depot per character, shared by every terminal: what you leave at the Outpost is waiting in Dustline.
