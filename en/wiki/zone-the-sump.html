Overview

PROVISIONAL. A drained reservoir gone back to marsh north of Frontier Road: dry cracked banks to the west, peat and reeds in the middle, standing water and half-sunk wrecks to the east. War drones circle a drowned VDI pump station whose open hatch leads down into the Pump Works.
