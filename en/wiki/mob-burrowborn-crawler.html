Lore

Hand-sized at birth, dog-sized at a year. Crawlers feel footsteps through stone long before they see anything.

Behaviour

SWARM SKITTER BITE CALL FLEE

  • Notices you within 6.5 m (it can sense up to 9 m) and remembers your last position for 6 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 9 m.
  • At 30% health it runs for 3 s — and comes back.
  • It fights from 0–1.2 m.

How to fight it

  • Weak alone, dangerous in numbers: pull them one at a time or kill them fast.
  • A hurt Crawler calls every Crawler nearby onto you.
  • Badly wounded Crawlers run for the nest — and come back.
