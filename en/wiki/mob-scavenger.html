Lore

Miners who were laid off, deserted or never signed on. They strip what the Outpost throws away and whatever an operator drops.

Behaviour

LOOT SHOUT SLASH GANG UP

  • Notices you within 8 m (it can sense up to 8 m) and remembers your last position for 3 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 7 m.
  • It fights from 0–1.5 m.

How to fight it

  • Hit one and the camp hears it: scavengers shout for each other and come together.
  • They are only people: no armour plates, no telegraphs — a fair fight one on one.
