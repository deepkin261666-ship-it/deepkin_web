Overview

Below the Deep Seal: The Dark Stratum (the Phase 11 floor two), narrow and dark, with a thermal vent, a soft-rock face to drill, a scavenger and ferrous samples. The shaft's lift cage climbs back to floor one; a stair pit drops to floor three.
