Overview

PROVISIONAL. An old open-cast salt and ore pit in the white flats south of Frontier Road: terraces of rust-red rock, a haul road down to the floor 30 m below, wrecked trucks and a machine graveyard, Castoff salvage crews, Rustbound robots still working the crusher. A sealed adit on the floor leads down — not yet surveyed.
