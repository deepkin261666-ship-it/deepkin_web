The classes in depth

Choosing a class

  1. Choose your discipline: four cards with a playstyle fingerprint (durability, melee, ranged, mobility, tech), the class power and passive, and the skills it trains fast. Hold Tab to compare all four. D opens the full dossier.
  2. Choose your starting weapon: every class's starter is offered. ★ RECOMMENDED are the ones your discipline trains fastest (TRAINS ×1.5, the first is pre-selected); OTHER STARTERS train at ×0.5 and say so. Enter deploys you with it in the Outpost gear crate.

Weapons are never locked: the class only sets how fast you train each one, and you can swap at any time.
