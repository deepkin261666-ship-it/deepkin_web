Lore

Three heads on one small, swollen body. Nobody knows what it was before the shaft breathed on it. They come in packs and they squeal for each other.

Behaviour

SWARM SQUEAL TRIPLE BITE SCATTER

  • Notices you within 13.5 m (it can sense up to 16.5 m) and remembers your last position for 7 s after losing you.
  • Pack hunter: hit one and the others nearby join in.
  • When hurt it calls every one of its kind within 10 m.
  • At 25% health it runs for 2.5 s — and comes back.
  • It fights from 0–1.2 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Triple Bite — 1.6 m reach in a 100° arc in front of it. It only lands if you are still inside when the wind-up ends: step out or behind it. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • One is nothing; the pack is the danger. A hurt one squeals and every brood nearby runs at you.
  • All three heads bite at once: the red arc in front of it is where.
  • Badly hurt, it scatters for a moment and comes back.
