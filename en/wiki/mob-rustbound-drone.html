Lore

A gun drone on a patrol loop its controller stopped updating years ago. It keeps its distance and fires bursts. Provisional lore.

Behaviour

HOVER KEEP DISTANCE BURST PATROL

  • Notices you within 11 m (it can sense up to 13 m) and remembers your last position for 6 s after losing you.
  • Predator: once it has your scent it follows you far, up to 22 m from home.
  • It fights from 5–9 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Burst — A glowing strip 0.8 m wide, from 2.5 to 11 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • It hangs back and fires a burst along a glowing line: step off the line or put a wall between you.
  • It needs a clear line to shoot: pillars and crates block it.
  • Fragile: close the distance and it goes down fast.
