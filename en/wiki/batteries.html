Spare cells

Carry spare cells in your pouch or worn backpack (never inside a spare bag) and the server moves charge from the fullest spare into the worn cell at 1 energy a second, with no loss. Regen pauses in combat (5 s after the last hit given or taken): the spares are a reserve between fights, not a heal. Spares keep their charge across logins. The Outpost Gear Crate gives three cells.

Recharge at the Alchemist

Vesko refills one empty cell (charge at or below 5%) for 30 carried Metal + 1 × Lumenita; the same cell comes back full. It shows as a blue service row under BUY that names what is missing.

A new cell costs 250 Metal at Pell: more than eight recharges, so recharging is the cheap way to keep a cell and a new one is for when you have no Lumenita or want another spare.

Where Lumenita comes from: Pit Wyrm (100%), Greater Dragon (80%), Ravager (50%), Dragon (50%), Tribal Bug (45%), Ripper (25%), Castoff Vanguard (15%)

Prices and numbers are provisional balance and may change.
