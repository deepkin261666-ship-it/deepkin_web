Overview

PROVISIONAL. Vantage Deep Industries' Contract Site 3: an open-cast spiral cut sixty metres down to a bore head, its blue work lights still burning on a grid that never shut down. Castoff salvage crews camp on the rim, Rustbound machines keep the substation, Mutants climb out of the bowl. The spiral haul road runs down to the Upper Benches (an abandoned VDI shift camp, a stalled convoy), the Lower Benches (survey borers, the first Lumenita, a Mutant den) and the Rig: Bore 1, still drilling on its schedule and guarded by Rustbound elites and turrets. Throw Breaker 3 at the rim substation and the whole pit goes dark: the turrets and the bore stop, the machines keep hunting. The Ruins gate on the west rim is sealed.
