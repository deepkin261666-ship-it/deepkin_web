Lore

A wall-fed sentry gun on the hideout's grid. When the grid drops, so does it. Provisional lore.

Behaviour

TRACK VOLLEY GO DARK

  • Notices you within 12 m (it can sense up to 14 m) and remembers your last position for 3 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 0 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 0–13 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Volley — A glowing strip 0.9 m wide, from 1.5 to 13 m. It flies along the locked facing, hits the first operator on the line and stops at the first wall: step off the strip. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • It never moves: it turns and fires a volley along a glowing line. Walls and crates block it.
  • It runs on the floor's power: in a blackout it goes dark and stops shooting - that is the moment to push.
  • Kill it from behind cover, or rush it between volleys.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 0 m from home.
