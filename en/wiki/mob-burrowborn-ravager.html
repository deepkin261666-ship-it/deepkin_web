Lore

A Crawler that never stopped growing. Its hide has mineralised into plates; the green glands still glow under them.

In the game

Behaviour

LUMBER CHARGE MAUL SWEEP SLAM GUARD

  • Notices you within 6.5 m (it can sense up to 8 m) and remembers your last position for 4 s after losing you.
  • Territorial: it never chases past 12 m from home; pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • It fights from 0–1.8 m.

Every ability is telegraphed: it winds up first, so you can read it.

  • Charge — A red lane 1.8 m wide lights up in front of it when you are 4–8 m away. It throws its weight along the locked facing, stops at the first wall or the first operator in the lane, and that operator takes the hit: step off the lane sideways. It needs line of sight to start. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Claw Sweep — A 140° fan, 2.8 m long, from the mouth along the facing it locks at the start. It needs a clear line from the creature: pillars, columns and ruins block it. Get to its flank or behind stone. Reduced by your Armor.
  • Slam — It rears up and the ground glows in a 2.4 m ring: step out within 1 s. Reduced by your Armor.

How to fight it

  • The end of the Shallows belongs to it: it waits in the east chamber in front of the way down to C-3.
  • Keep your distance and it charges: a red lane lights up in front of it — step off the lane.
  • Close in, it sweeps its claws across a wide arc, and before it slams it rears up while the ground glows red: step out of the ring.
  • It will not leave its territory: pull it to the edge and it turns back.
  • Fight near pillars, columns or ruins: its cone needs a clear line, so stone is cover.
  • If it goes badly, run: it gives up 12 m from home.
