Drones

Launch them from the cargo: a lamp, a gun, a scout you pilot or a bomb you fly in. They draw from your battery.

Firefly Light Drone

A palm-sized quad with a floodlamp under its belly. Deployed, it hovers over your shoulder and lights the ground around you for up to three minutes, drawing 0.25 energy a second from your worn cell. Press its key again to call it back. Relay Nine stock; Rask sells a few in Dustline.

Weight
0.9 kg
Tier
T1

Hammerhead Strike Drone

A heavier single-use FPV quad carrying twice the charge. Launch it and stand still at its tablet: you fly it in first person (movement keys; up to 55 m away) until it hits something or you detonate it (Space or its key again). The blast hurts every hostile within 4.5 m (75 at the centre, less at the edge; elites and bosses shrug off most of it). The drone is spent. Draws 1 energy a second while it flies, 45 seconds at most. Relay Nine stock; Rask sells it in Dustline.

Contract Drive

A scuffed USB stick with a wax seal over the port: an employer's payment in crypto, 800 Metal. Insert it at the BANK app of any 261.6.66 terminal (INSERT DRIVE) to credit your account; the drive is wiped and kept by the bank.

Weight
0.02 kg

Empanada

A baked half-moon of dough around spiced meat, still warm from Auntie Rhea's oven. Eat it to restore 60 health over 20 seconds; one meal at a time. Nobody asks what the meat was.

Expedition Service

A Vantage Deep Industries field tablet with one paid excavation on it. Use it on open ground of a wild map (not on a road, in a settlement or near a passage) and a VDI drillship comes down from the sky a few seconds later, thirteen metres ahead of you. You are its Owner: walk through its door, run the VDI Excavation software at the terminal, load batteries and gas cans into its reserves and take it down, floor by floor. "We go where the world ends." Spent when the ship is called.

Weight
0.6 kg
